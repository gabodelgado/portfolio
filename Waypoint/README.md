# 🧭 Waypoint

A personal finance planner: enter what you earn and what you spend, and see what you can save per paycheck and how long a goal will take.

**[▶ Try it live](https://gabodelgado.github.io/portfolio/Waypoint/)**

![Waypoint](screenshot.png)

## What it does

- **Income:** hourly pay, hours per week and pay frequency (weekly, biweekly or monthly), plus any other monthly income.
- **Taxes:** you enter your real tax rate, and the app never uses an estimate. Pick one of 60 countries (and a state or region for the US and Spain) to get notes on local taxes, such as the extra New York City, Ohio municipal and Pennsylvania local income taxes.
- **Expenses:** fixed and variable monthly expenses, and you can add your own.
- **Your plan:** gross annual and monthly income, net monthly income, expenses, savings per month and per paycheck, a visual comparison, and a projection for 1 and 5 years. It updates live as you type.
- **Savings goal:** set an amount, and optionally a date. With a date, Waypoint tells you how much to save each paycheck and whether you're on track. Without one, it estimates when you'll get there.
- **Nothing gets lost:** everything you type is saved in the browser.
- **Two languages:** Spanish and English, with numbers written the way each one expects. Number fields add thousands separators as you type.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks or build step. The only external resource is the Cormorant Garamond font from Google Fonts.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser.
